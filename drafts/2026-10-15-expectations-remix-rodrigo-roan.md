# Olivia Rodrigo and Chappell Roan Team Up on "Expectations" Remix

Olivia Rodrigo has teamed up with Chappell Roan on a remix of "Expectations," a track from her third album, *You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love*. The remix arrives Thursday, October 15, at 5 p.m. PT, alongside a music video featuring both artists.

Rodrigo announced the collaboration on Instagram on Tuesday, October 13, with a photo of herself and Roan posing on a yellow convertible. Her caption read "beyond excited to announce 'expectations' featuring Chappell Roan!!! song and video out Thursday!!!!" The reveal followed a teaser the day before, in which a woman's voice says "Ooh, Olivia does remixes now." Fans had already suspected Roan was the voice, so the announcement confirmed what many had guessed.

For Roan, the remix is her first credited feature and the first official collaboration between the two. The track was produced by Dan Nigro, who also produced the original "Expectations." He has worked with both artists for years, which is one reason the pairing made sense to fans who followed their careers. Both women are Grammy winners for Best New Artist, a shared line that rarely shows up in a single team-up announcement.

The two have shared stages before. In 2022, Roan opened a show on Rodrigo's Sour Tour, and she later supported the GUTS World Tour. In 2024 they sang "Hot to Go!" together in Los Angeles. Roan also performed at Rodrigo's inaugural Daisy Chain Fields festival in Irvine, California, in August, an event that raised $20 million for charities supporting women and girls.

Roan's voice was already on Rodrigo's records. She sings background vocals on "lacy" and "obsessed" from GUTS, according to reports from 2024. Those parts were easy to miss unless you knew to listen for them. The friendship also runs deeper than studio work. In October 2024, Rodrigo told Billboard that Roan "actually gives me so much advice on taking care of myself and being me in an industry that's so overwhelming sometimes."

"Expectations" is the fourth single from *You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love*, following "Drop Dead," "The Cure" and "Stupid Song." The uptempo original is the 12th track on the album, which came out in June and debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200. The remix video, directed by Bardia Zeinali, premieres with the song on Thursday.

For years Roan's voice was the part of Rodrigo's records that fans had to listen closely to find. On Thursday her name goes on the single itself, next to Rodrigo's.
