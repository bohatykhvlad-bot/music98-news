Olivia Rodrigo and Chappell Roan have released a remix of "expectations," a song from Rodrigo's third studio album, you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love. The remix arrived Thursday, October 15, through Interscope Records, with an official music video directed by Bardia Zeinali. It is Roan's first credited feature on a song.

The original track came out in June on the album, which debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200. It is a dance synth-pop song about refusing to settle, and its lyrics describe a narrator who has learned from past mistakes and no longer accepts a partner who is unreliable. Daniel Nigro, who helmed the original, also produced the remix.

Rodrigo first hinted at the collaboration on Monday, October 12, when she posted an audio clip of a female voice saying, "Ooh, Olivia does remixes now." Fans suspected the voice belonged to Roan. On October 13, Rodrigo confirmed the feature in an Instagram post shared with Roan, writing that she was "beyond excited" to announce the song and video.

The two singers have known each other for years. Roan opened a show on Rodrigo's Sour Tour in 2022 and later supported the Guts World Tour. In August, she played Daisy Chain Fields, the all-women festival Rodrigo organized in Irvine, California, performing just before Rodrigo's headlining set. The festival raised $20 million for ten nonprofits that work with women and girls, including a $10 million match from philanthropist Melinda French Gates.

The video features both artists and was released alongside the single. Bardia Zeinali is known for work with Katseye, Sabrina Carpenter and Doja Cat.

The cover art, also used for the remix, was shot by Phoenix Johnson. It shows Rodrigo and Roan posing on a retro convertible in 1980s-style outfits.

Rodrigo is on the road with The Unraveled Tour, which has sold out. Roan is preparing her own benefit, The Super Graphic Spectacular, two nights at the Shrine Auditorium in Los Angeles on October 21 and 22. Caroline Polachek, Lola Young, MUNA and Tinashe are on the bill, and net proceeds go to Roan's Midwest Princess Project, which supports trans youth and LGBTQ+ communities.

It is also Roan's first release since "The Subway," her last solo single. Her name now sits beside Rodrigo's on the record, after years of sharing stages on Rodrigo's tours.
