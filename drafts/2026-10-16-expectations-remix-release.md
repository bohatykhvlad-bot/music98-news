Olivia Rodrigo and Chappell Roan have released a remix of "expectations," a song from Rodrigo's third studio album, You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love. The single arrived on Thursday, October 15, through Interscope Records, and it marks the first feature of Roan's career on a song.

The original version of "expectations" came out in June with the album. The record debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with 485,000 first-week units, according to the label, which called it the biggest solo debut of 2026. The original is a new wave-inspired dance track about setting high standards in relationships. Its lyrics describe a narrator who refuses to settle for a partner who is unreliable or passive, and who has learned from past mistakes.

Rodrigo announced the collaboration on October 13 in an Instagram post shared alongside Roan. Days earlier, she had teased the project with a short audio clip in which a female voice said, "Ooh, Olivia does remixes now." Fans suspected the voice belonged to Roan. Rodrigo wrote that she was "beyond excited" to announce the song and its video.

The two singers have known each other for years. Roan opened a show on Rodrigo's Sour Tour in 2022 and later supported the Guts World Tour. In August, she performed at Daisy Chain Fields, Rodrigo's inaugural women-led festival in Irvine, California. Roan played just before Rodrigo's headlining set. Rodrigo curated the all-female lineup, which also included Doechii, Mitski, Garbage and the Breeders. The event raised $20 million for ten nonprofits that work with women and girls.

Daniel Nigro produced the remix. He also worked on the original, and he has collaborated with both artists for years.

The official music video was directed by Bardia Zeinali and features both artists. The label says Zeinali has also worked with Katseye, Sabrina Carpenter and Doja Cat. The video was released alongside the single.

The cover art was shot by Phoenix Johnson. It shows Rodrigo and Roan posing on a vintage convertible in 1980s-style outfits.

Rodrigo is currently on her sold-out Unraveled Tour. Roan has her own busy schedule as well. On October 21 and 22, she will host The Super Graphic Spectacular in Los Angeles, a two-day charity event with Caroline Polachek, Lola Young, MUNA and Tinashe on the bill.

Roan's most recent solo single before this was "The Subway," released in July 2025. This remix is her first credited feature, and her name now sits beside Rodrigo's on the release.
