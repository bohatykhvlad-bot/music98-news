# RAYE Announces One-Night Orchestral Show at Royal Albert Hall

## Article body

RAYE will return to London's Royal Albert Hall on October 23 to perform her second album, *This Music May Contain Hope.*, in full with an orchestra. The one-night show, titled *This Symphony May Contain Hope.*, will feature her band, the Heritage Orchestra and Flames Collective. It was announced just two weeks before the concert, with presale opening October 13 and general ticket sales beginning October 15. Rather than adding another stop to her arena tour, she has booked a separate evening built around the new record.

She first performed at the Royal Albert Hall with an orchestra in September 2023, months after releasing *My 21st Century Blues*. Accompanied by the Heritage Orchestra and Flames Collective, she sang material from the debut album she had spent years fighting to make. The concert was filmed for *Live at the Royal Albert Hall*, later nominated for a Grammy in the Best Music Film category. She brought her *My 21st Century Symphony* show to The O2 the following year, again performing with the orchestra and choir.

The road to those concerts was far from straightforward. In 2021, after years of releasing singles and writing for other artists, RAYE publicly criticised Polydor for holding back her debut album. She left the label and eventually released *My 21st Century Blues* independently in 2023. "Escapism." with 070 Shake gave her a first UK No. 1 single, and in 2024 she won six BRIT Awards in one night, including Album of the Year and Songwriter of the Year. For a singer once unable to get her own album released, performing that music with an orchestra was a particularly striking turnaround.

Her follow-up, released in March, stretches across jazz, soul, R&B and ambitious pop arrangements. RAYE structured *This Music May Contain Hope.* around four seasons, with the early songs dwelling on difficult experiences before the later material turns towards optimism. "Click Clack Symphony." features Hans Zimmer, while "Nightingale Lane." draws on the jazz and soul singing that has long been central to her music. The new Royal Albert Hall date is billed as a performance of the complete album, with the songs kept together rather than pulled out as isolated hits.

Alongside the announcement, she released a new performance of "I Will Overcome." recorded at Abbey Road Studios with the London Symphony Orchestra. That is a different ensemble from the Heritage Orchestra booked for the concert, although both recordings place her songwriting in an orchestral setting. The song had already appeared at the opening night of her 2026 tour in Łódź, Poland, where she introduced several tracks from the then-unreleased album in January. Listeners can now hear one of those early live songs performed with the London Symphony Orchestra before she brings the entire album to Royal Albert Hall.
