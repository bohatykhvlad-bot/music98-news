# RAYE Announces One-Night Orchestral Show at Royal Albert Hall

**Status:** LIVE on music98.news. Corrected copy published and verified in the authenticated editor.

**Excerpt (plain-text rendering of the first sentence):** RAYE will return to London's Royal Albert Hall on October 23 to perform her second album in full.

## Article body

RAYE will return to London's Royal Albert Hall on October 23 to perform her second album in full. The one-night concert, *This Symphony May Contain Hope.*, will feature her band, the Heritage Orchestra and Flames Collective choir. Both ensembles accompanied her at the same venue three years ago, when she performed songs from her first album with specially written arrangements for a full orchestra. Tickets go on sale October 15.

Released in March, *This Music May Contain Hope.* is divided into four seasons. Its songs begin with heartbreak and isolation before gradually moving towards hope. RAYE told Apple Music she wanted to make something that could comfort listeners during difficult times. The album mixes jazz, soul and pop, with Hans Zimmer contributing to "Click Clack Symphony." It also became her first album to reach No. 1 in the UK. There are quieter passages, too, where her voice carries the songs without a large arrangement behind it.

The concert announcement also brought a new Abbey Road Studios performance of "I Will Overcome." with the London Symphony Orchestra. In an interview with ELLE, RAYE called the song "big and dramatic" and explained its central lyric. She admits that she isn't okay but tells herself she will get there somehow. It's not a victory song about leaving the worst behind. She's still in the middle of it, which makes the large orchestral arrangement an unusual match for such a private confession.

Her previous Royal Albert Hall performance was built around *My 21st Century Blues*, an album she had struggled for years to release. The show was filmed, and the resulting concert movie later received a Grammy nomination for Best Music Film. She also brought an orchestral version of the production to The O2 the following year. RAYE has spoken about how much she enjoys changing arrangements onstage, sometimes extending an introduction or taking a song in a different direction from its studio recording.

Before she could release that first album, RAYE spent years writing songs for other artists, including Beyoncé and Charli XCX, while putting out EPs and dance-pop singles of her own. She has said she wanted to make R&B but had little creative control at her record label, Polydor. After publicly criticizing the delay, she left in 2021. Working independently allowed her to explore the jazz and soul influences that had received less attention in her earlier music.

Her breakout independent single, "Escapism." with 070 Shake, describes someone trying to escape the pain of a breakup through drinking and nights out. Its darker sound was a departure from RAYE's earlier dance-pop releases, and it became her first UK No. 1. The success brought a larger audience to a more personal side of her writing. At her first orchestral show, she reworked songs that people already knew. Now she is returning with an album that has that scale built into the music from the start.
