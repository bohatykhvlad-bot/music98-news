# RAYE Announces One-Night Orchestral Show at Royal Albert Hall

**Status:** LIVE on music98.news. Corrected copy published and verified in the authenticated editor.

**Excerpt (plain-text rendering of the first sentence):** RAYE will perform her second album in full at London's Royal Albert Hall on October 23.

## Article body

RAYE will perform her second album in full at London's Royal Albert Hall on October 23. The one-night concert, *This Symphony May Contain Hope.*, will feature her band, the Heritage Orchestra and Flames Collective choir. She began playing new songs on tour before the album came out, using the live shows to work out how they should sound on the finished record. Tickets go on sale October 15 at 10am.

Released in March, *This Music May Contain Hope.* follows four seasons, moving from loneliness towards hope. RAYE told Apple Music she hoped the songs would help listeners through difficult times. For "Click Clack Symphony.", she sent Hans Zimmer a rough demo and asked if he wanted to collaborate. They exchanged ideas until the recording included an orchestra. She called the finished song "unapologetically cinematic." The full album went to No. 1 in the UK.

The announcement included a new Abbey Road Studios performance of "I Will Overcome." with the London Symphony Orchestra. Earlier this year, RAYE played the song for an ELLE journalist and called it "big and dramatic." She explained lyrics about how people hide their pain, followed by an admission that she is struggling but believes she will get through it. The song follows a short opening scene about a woman alone in Paris.

Her previous Royal Albert Hall concert put songs from *My 21st Century Blues* into new arrangements for orchestra and choir. That 2023 performance was released as a live album and a concert film, which later earned a Grammy nomination for Best Music Film. She brought a similar production to The O2 the following year. Unlike that earlier show, which reworked existing recordings, the new concert is built around material that already incorporates orchestral arrangements in the studio.

Her first album had taken years to reach the public. RAYE wrote songs for other artists, including Beyoncé and Charli XCX, while Polydor, her record label at the time, released mostly EPs and dance-pop singles under her own name. She has said she wanted to make R&B but had little creative control over her own music. In 2021, frustrated by the continued delay, she criticized the label publicly and then left to work independently.

Her first big independent hit was "Escapism.", a collaboration with 070 Shake. The song follows someone trying to numb the pain of a breakup through alcohol, drugs and nights out, far removed from the lighter dance-pop singles that had dominated her earlier work. In January 2023, it became her first UK No. 1 single. *My 21st Century Blues* followed the next month, with the jazz, soul and R&B influences she had long wanted to explore. RAYE called the chart success proof that she had been right to back herself.

On *This Music May Contain Hope.*, RAYE wrote from a different place. She told ELLE she had composed most of it sober and no longer used alcohol to bury difficult feelings. Its opening autumn chapter explores the urge to fix loneliness with a drink or a relationship. She spoke about learning to spend an evening by herself, something she once found difficult. "I don't want someone else to come and fix me," she said.
