# RAYE Announces One-Night Orchestral Show at Royal Albert Hall

**Status:** LIVE on music98.news. Corrected copy published and verified in the authenticated editor.

## Article body

RAYE will perform her second album in full at London's Royal Albert Hall on October 23, backed by the orchestra and choir from her previous symphonic concert. The one-night show, titled *This Symphony May Contain Hope.*, will bring her band together with the Heritage Orchestra and Flames Collective choir. Instead of a set spanning her career, the concert will follow the new record from beginning to end. Tickets go on sale October 15. It will be her second orchestral concert at the venue.

Released in March, *This Music May Contain Hope.* is divided into four seasons, with songs that move from heartbreak and loneliness towards a more hopeful ending. RAYE told Apple Music she wanted to create something that might help listeners through difficult periods. The album moves between jazz, soul and pop, often on a grander scale than her earlier work. "Click Clack Symphony." features Hans Zimmer, while other tracks leave more space for her voice. The record became her first UK No. 1 album.

The concert announcement also brought a new performance of "I Will Overcome." recorded at Abbey Road Studios with the London Symphony Orchestra. RAYE told ELLE earlier this year that the song grew out of a period when she needed to remind herself she could get through difficult experiences. It appears near the beginning of the album, establishing a theme of perseverance before the story moves through its four seasons. The Abbey Road recording gives the song a new orchestral arrangement.

At her 2023 Royal Albert Hall concert, RAYE performed songs from her debut with the Heritage Orchestra and Flames Collective choir. The concert was filmed, later earning a Grammy nomination for Best Music Film, and also released as a live album. She took the same orchestral format to The O2 the following year. She has spoken about enjoying the freedom to change arrangements onstage, rather than making each performance sound exactly like the studio recordings.

Before releasing her debut, RAYE spent years writing songs for other artists, including Beyoncé and Charli XCX, while releasing EPs and dance-pop singles under her own name. She has said she wanted to make R&B, but her record label, Polydor, gave her little creative control. After publicly criticizing the delay of her debut album, she left the label and finally released *My 21st Century Blues* independently. The record gave her a chance to make room for the jazz and soul influences that mattered to her.

The debut produced the UK No. 1 single "Escapism." with 070 Shake and led to a record six BRIT Awards in one night. Her second album takes a different approach, telling a longer story across its songs and making orchestral arrangements part of its sound from the start. At Royal Albert Hall, the musicians who helped rework her debut will return for a full performance of the new record. Unlike the earlier show, this concert will follow the new album's story rather than return to the debut's biggest songs.
