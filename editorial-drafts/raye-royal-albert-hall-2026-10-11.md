# RAYE Announces One-Night Orchestral Show at Royal Albert Hall

## Article body

RAYE will return to London's Royal Albert Hall on October 23 for a one-night orchestral concert built around her second album. The show, titled *This Symphony May Contain Hope.*, will present *This Music May Contain Hope.* in full. Instead of playing a selection of singles, she will perform the whole record with her band, an orchestra and a choir. Presales begin October 13, with general tickets available later that week.

At her previous Royal Albert Hall concert, she performed songs from *My 21st Century Blues*. The Heritage Orchestra and Flames Collective choir accompanied songs from the album, and the performance was released as both a concert film and live album. The film later received a Grammy nomination. She also brought the orchestral format to The O2 in London. In interviews, she has described enjoying the freedom to extend introductions and change arrangements onstage rather than reproduce the studio recordings exactly.

RAYE had already built a career writing for other artists before releasing her debut album. She signed a publishing deal at 16 and contributed songs to Beyoncé and Charli XCX while putting out dance-pop singles and EPs under her own name. She later told the Recording Academy that R&B was the music she wanted to make, but that she had little creative control at Polydor. Although she had signed a deal for four albums, none was released during her time there. She publicly challenged the delay in 2021 and left the label.

Her debut, *My 21st Century Blues*, eventually arrived. "Escapism." with 070 Shake became her first UK No. 1 single, and she went on to win a record six BRIT Awards in one night. Album of the Year was among her wins. The record made room for jazz and soul alongside R&B and pop, revealing a side of her writing that had not always come through in the earlier dance singles.

Her second album is structured around four seasons, moving from heartbreak toward hope. She has said she wanted to make music that could help people through difficult times. "Click Clack Symphony.", featuring Hans Zimmer, has a cinematic sweep, while "Nightingale Lane." leans into jazz and soul. The Royal Albert Hall performance will follow the album's four-season arc rather than selecting only its most familiar songs. The record became her first UK No. 1 album.

Alongside the announcement, she released a new performance of "I Will Overcome." recorded at Abbey Road Studios with the London Symphony Orchestra. She first sang the song at the opening night of her current tour in Poland, while the album was still unreleased. The Abbey Road video is a separate orchestral recording, not footage from the upcoming concert. For that show, she will return to the orchestra and choir that accompanied her earlier symphony.
