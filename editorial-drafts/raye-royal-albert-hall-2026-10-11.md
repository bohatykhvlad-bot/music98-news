# RAYE Announces One-Night Orchestral Show at Royal Albert Hall

**Status:** LIVE on music98.news. Corrected copy published and verified in the authenticated editor.

**Excerpt (plain-text rendering of the first sentence):** RAYE will return to London's Royal Albert Hall on October 23 for a one-night orchestral concert built around material from her latest album.

## Article body

RAYE will return to London's Royal Albert Hall on October 23 for a one-night orchestral concert built around material from her latest album. The show, titled *This Symphony May Contain Hope.*, will feature her band, the Heritage Orchestra and Flames Collective choir. Several of those songs were introduced live when the tour opened in Poland in January, and the new material has remained in the set since then. Tickets go on sale October 15 at 10am.

Released in March, *This Music May Contain Hope.* is arranged around four seasons, starting in a darker place and moving toward hope. RAYE told Apple Music she hoped the songs would help listeners through difficult times. For "Click Clack Symphony.", she sent Hans Zimmer a rough demo and asked if he wanted to work on it. The two went back and forth until the finished recording included an orchestra. She later called the track "unapologetically cinematic." The album reached No. 1 in the UK.

The concert announcement arrived with a new Abbey Road Studios performance of "I Will Overcome." with the London Symphony Orchestra. In January, RAYE played the then-unreleased album version for ELLE and called it "big and dramatic." She described a verse in which the narrator stops hiding her pain, admits things are not okay and still believes she will get through it. On the album, the song follows a spoken introduction about a woman alone in Paris.

The October concert is her second symphonic project. In 2023, RAYE rearranged songs from *My 21st Century Blues* for the same venue with orchestra and choir. That performance later became a live album and concert film. A similar production followed at The O2 the next year, and the film later received a Grammy nomination. The orchestra and choir from that 2023 concert are returning together for the new show in October.

Before releasing an album of her own, RAYE spent years writing songs for artists including Beyoncé and Charli XCX. Polydor signed her as a teenager, but seven years passed without a full-length release despite several EPs and singles. She has said she wanted to make R&B, while the label steered her toward dance-pop and left little room for creative control. In 2021, after publicly criticizing the delays, she left Polydor and began releasing music independently.

In January 2023, "Escapism." with 070 Shake became her first UK No. 1 single. The song turned a breakup into a dark story of alcohol, drugs and nights out, a sharp departure from the dance-pop singles that had defined much of her earlier career. Her debut album followed the next month, finally putting jazz, soul and R&B at the center of her own record. RAYE later described the chart success as proof that backing herself had been the right decision.

RAYE told ELLE she wrote most of the record sober and no longer used alcohol to bury or numb difficult feelings. The album's autumn section starts with loneliness and the temptation to reach for alcohol or a relationship as a quick fix. She also spoke about learning to spend an evening by herself, something she once found difficult, and becoming more comfortable in her own company. "I don't want someone else to come and fix me," she said.
