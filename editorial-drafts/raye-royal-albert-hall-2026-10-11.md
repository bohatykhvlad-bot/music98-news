# RAYE Announces One-Night Orchestral Show at Royal Albert Hall

**Status:** LIVE on music98.news. Corrected copy published and verified in the authenticated editor.

**Excerpt (plain-text rendering of the first sentence):** RAYE will return to London's Royal Albert Hall on October 23 to perform her second album in full.

## Article body

RAYE will return to London's Royal Albert Hall on October 23 to perform her second album in full. The one-night concert, *This Symphony May Contain Hope.*, will reunite her with the Heritage Orchestra and Flames Collective, the orchestra and choir from her previous symphonic performance at the venue. Her own band will join them, bringing all three groups together for the newly released material. Tickets go on sale October 15.

Released in March, *This Music May Contain Hope.* is divided into four seasons. Its songs begin with heartbreak and loneliness before gradually turning towards hope. RAYE told Apple Music she wanted to make something listeners could turn to during difficult times. The songs move between jazz, soul and pop, and Hans Zimmer also appears on "Click Clack Symphony." It was her first album to reach No. 1 in the UK.

The announcement came with a new Abbey Road Studios performance of "I Will Overcome." with the London Symphony Orchestra. Earlier this year, RAYE told ELLE that the song was "big and dramatic" and spoke about lyrics in which she acknowledges that she is struggling but believes she will get through it. On the album, the track follows a short introduction. It establishes the story's starting point before the music moves through its darker chapters and, eventually, towards hope.

Her earlier Royal Albert Hall concert was built around *My 21st Century Blues*, which she had struggled for years to release. That concert was filmed, and the resulting movie later received a Grammy nomination for Best Music Film. A live album followed, and she also brought the symphonic production to The O2. RAYE has described how much she enjoys changing arrangements onstage, sometimes extending an introduction or taking a song in a different direction from its studio version.

Before she could release her first album, RAYE spent years writing for other artists, including Beyoncé and Charli XCX, while putting out EPs and dance-pop singles under her own name. She has said she wanted to make R&B but had little creative control at her record label, Polydor. After publicly criticizing the delays, she left in 2021. As an independent artist, she could finally explore the jazz and soul influences that had received less attention in her earlier releases.

One of her first independent successes was "Escapism.", a collaboration with 070 Shake about trying to numb the pain of a breakup through drinking and nights out. The song's jagged electronic sound marked a departure from her earlier singles, and it reached No. 1 in the UK before *My 21st Century Blues* was released. Her new album also deals with difficult experiences, but its four-season structure leads the songs toward a more hopeful ending. At Royal Albert Hall, she will perform that progression in full, with the musicians who joined her for her first symphonic concert.
