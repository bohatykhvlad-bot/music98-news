## Selective full-review corrections, verified October 4, 2026

- **Lyric and video interpretation.** Shazam's transcribed bridge imagines an eastbound I-40 drive and accepts that the boyfriend's mind will not change. This is not proof that the narrator has already driven away in the recording. The video shows her actually leaving with Lambert. https://www.shazam.com/song/1869436839/choosin-texas ; previous registry https://www.fortworth.com/articles/post/choosin-texas-was-filmed-in-fort-worth/
- **Dolly's original intent.** Dolly Parton's official retrospective expressly dates "I Will Always Love You" to her professional departure from Porter Wagoner's show. The article distinguishes that origin from the lyric's broader farewell meaning; it does not describe the original song as an autobiographical romantic breakup. https://www.dollyparton.com/news/dolly-leaves-porter-wagoner-show
- **Kitty Wells at home.** Langley told Rolling Stone that she uses Wells's "It Wasn't God Who Made Honky Tonk Angels" as the alarm on her smart speaker. Treat as her stated habit in April 2026, not independently observed routine. https://ca.rollingstone.com/ella-langley-choosin-texas-interview/
- **Music-listening preference.** Billboard Women in Music 2026 filmed interview includes Langley's first-person statement that she attends to instrumentation before lyrics when first listening to a song. The article accurately attributes this preference, without claiming documentary evidence it determined production choices. https://www.dailymotion.com/video/xa5rusq
- **Actual instrumentation and listening interpretation.** Apple-owned Shazam album credits document drums/percussion, bass, electric guitar, acoustic guitar/mandolin, and steel guitar. Music analysis of their audible interaction is written as critical listening, not presented as an artist quote or a fact about recording intent. The Guardian's July 29 2026 analysis independently characterizes the song as midtempo, relaxed, retro, guitar- and pedal-steel-forward, with smooth pop-format compatibility. https://www.shazam.com/song/1869436839/choosin-texas ; https://www.theguardian.com/music/2026/jul/29/ella-langley-choosin-texas-song-of-the-year
- **Outro personnel.** Shazam credits Ella Langley as vocalist and Charlie Worsham on acoustic guitar for the closing "Froggy Went A Courtin'". Do not say Ella is alone or unaccompanied. https://www.shazam.com/song/1869437040/froggy-went-a-courtin-outro ; family-reunion/piano reminiscence confirmed in Rolling Stone https://ca.rollingstone.com/ella-langley-choosin-texas-interview/
