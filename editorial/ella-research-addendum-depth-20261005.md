# Ella longread research addendum – additional interview quotes and country-growth evidence

Prepared for the follow-up editorial rewrite. These citations belong in the private editorial registry, not in the published article.

1. Ella's own explanation of "Choosin' Texas" and its relatable emotional core. In Joseph Hudak's April 8, 2026 interview, Ella said "Everyone can relate to wanting something that doesn't want you back" (12 words), and discussed Stevie Nicks as a major songwriting influence. Primary direct interview: https://ca.rollingstone.com/ella-langley-choosin-texas-interview/ . The first quote can be used directly once, no claim that it establishes causal proof of commercial success.
2. Ella on how she selected the single: Billboard Women in Music filmed interview says "I honestly knew it was going to be the lead single" (11 words), while she explicitly did not predict the scale of the later success. Primary video transcript at https://www.dailymotion.com/video/xa5rusq , approximately 00:21-00:43.
3. Ella's intended album listening experience: same video, around 02:42-04:20, describes Dandelion as groovy, playable while cooking or spending time with friends; says she notices instrumentation before lyrics and planned album with advance visual boards and a large reference playlist. Paraphrase, do not exaggerate cause or assert she planned pop crossover.
4. Independent musical analysis of Choosin' Texas: Hit Songs Deconstructed explains a recurring swaying/lilting rhythmic motif seeded in the instrumental intro and echoed by vocal phrases, then strategic breaks before payoff points; a two-step feel supports dance-hall imagery. https://www.hitsongsdeconstructed.com/hsd_wire/choosin-texas-rhythmic-motif-technique/ . Explain in accessible language without claiming to have conducted independent waveform analysis.
5. General critical characterization of midtempo, retro-leaning pedal-steel and electric-guitar-led sound, and anecdotal broad online circulation: https://www.theguardian.com/music/2026/jul/29/ella-langley-choosin-texas-song-of-the-year . Secondary, do not treat critic inference as artist intent.
6. Independent streaming-market evidence: Luminate's 2024 Music Year-End Report states that the share of COUNTRY-GENRE U.S. and non-U.S. on-demand audio consumption taking place outside the US increased by 7.71 percentage points since 2020 (a shift of consumption share, NOT a 7.71% total-stream count increase). https://view.ceros.com/luminate-data/music-year-end-report-2024 . Use only qualitative interpretation in article if precise chart labels remain ambiguous.

Spotify official 2024 retrospective documents Shaboozey crossing national boundaries, topping charts including Sweden and featuring in non-country flagship playlists; Spotify's own editor identifies country growth outside the U.S. https://newsroom.spotify.com/2024-08-28/2024-songs-of-summer-reveal/ . This establishes cross-border reach but does not prove why any individual artist succeeded.
