Privacy

No accounts, no server-side store. Subscribe and desk drafts live in localStorage on this device. Chart audio is streamed from Apple (iTunes preview) or Deezer. Outbound Listen on Apple Music links use Apple’s affiliate parameters so a token can be added later without changing the layout.

How the Top 50 is built

On load we show a baked snapshot, then refresh Apple Music (US most played, 50) and Deezer (worldwide tracks, 50). Spotify Global and Billboard Hot 100 are compiled into the snapshot — they have no public CORS feed. Each position scores 51 minus rank. The page then re-sorts. Every row’s badge is the official Listen on Apple Music artwork from Apple’s Marketing Tools API, wrapped around an Apple Music song URL.
