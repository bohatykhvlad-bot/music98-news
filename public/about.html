About us

Music news without the noise

music98.news is an independent music publication: news, release reviews and a daily Top 50 chart. Short, sharp and to the point.

We cover what is actually happening in music right now — tours announced, albums dropped, charts moved — and we write it the way we would want to read it: quickly, honestly, with context. If a story can be told in three paragraphs, we tell it in three paragraphs.

What we publish

NewsIndustry stories that matter, reported the same week they happen.
ReleasesAlbums, EPs and singles we consider worth your listening time.
ChartsA daily Top 50 with real movement: who is rising, who is slipping, who holds.

How we work

Every post is checked before it goes out. We read the primary sources — label announcements, artist statements, chart data, official pages — and we link directly to the music so you can hear it yourself in one tap. When a story comes from another outlet, that outlet gets the credit.

If something has not been confirmed, we write it that way. A claim that only exists in a social post or an aggregator does not become a fact by being repeated, so it goes out as unconfirmed, and we name who has said nothing.

Release facts are kept in a registry. Dates, formats, track counts and spellings are verified against official pages and the music services before publication, and when a source and the artist's own catalogue disagree, the catalogue wins.

Album artwork and artist photos come from official press materials or from Creative Commons, with the credit shown on each post. If an image is yours and you want it taken down, write to us and it comes down, no formal notice needed. When we get something wrong, we fix it and say what changed.

The chart is compiled daily from streaming data and editorial scoring. Movement between days is tracked and shown openly — you always see why a track sits where it sits.

Who is behind it

music98.news is an independent project, and we hold our own pages to the same bar we hold the music we cover.

Get in touch

Editorial questions, press releases, corrections, rights and advertising all reach one address: info@music98.news. The Contacts page lists the tag for each.
