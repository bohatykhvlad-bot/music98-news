About us

Music news without the noise

music98.news is an independent music media: industry news, release reviews and a daily Top 50 chart. Short, sharp and to the point.

We cover what is actually happening in music right now — tours announced, albums dropped, charts moved — and we write it the way we would want to read it: quickly, honestly, with context. No endless intros, no filler paragraphs, no recycled press releases. If a story can be told in three paragraphs, we tell it in three paragraphs.

What we publish

NewsIndustry stories that matter, reported the same week they happen.
ReleasesAlbums, EPs and singles we consider worth your listening time.
ChartsA daily Top 50 with real movement: who is rising, who is slipping, who holds.

How we work

Every post is written by our editorial team. We read the primary sources — label announcements, artist statements, chart data — and we link directly to the music so you can hear it yourself in one tap. Album artwork and artist photos on this site are used either under license, from official press materials, or under their respective Creative Commons licenses with attribution to the photographer on each post.

The chart is compiled daily from streaming data and editorial scoring. Movement between days is tracked and shown openly — you always see why a track sits where it sits.

Who is behind it

music98.news is built and edited by a small independent team of music writers. We are readers of NME, Pitchfork and The Line of Best Fit ourselves — and we wanted a faster, cleaner daily read. That is the bar we hold our own pages to.

Get in touch: for editorial questions, press releases, corrections or partnership offers — write to info@music98.news. We read everything.
