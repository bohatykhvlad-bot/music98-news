About us

Music news without the noise

music98.news is an independent music publication covering news, releases, charts and live music discovery.

We focus on what is happening in music now — announcements, releases, chart movement and upcoming concerts — and try to make each page useful without padding it with unnecessary copy.

What we publish

NewsCurrent music-industry and artist stories with source checking and context.
ReleasesNew albums, EPs and singles with verified release information and direct listening links.
ChartsA regularly updated music98.news Top 50 with visible movement between updates.
ConcertsTicketmaster-powered event discovery by location, plus upcoming dates for popular artists.

How we work

We prefer primary sources: official artist and label announcements, verified catalogues, chart or platform data, ticketing data and direct statements. When another publication is the source of a story, we identify that source rather than presenting its reporting as our own.

Unconfirmed information is labelled as unconfirmed. Release dates, track information, credits and event details are checked against the strongest available source before publication.

Images are selected from official promotional material, licensed sources or other sources where use is permitted, with credits provided where available. Rights holders can request a correction or removal through the Contacts page.

Corrections

If a factual error is identified, we correct the current page rather than leaving information we know to be wrong. Readers, artists, labels, venues and other affected parties can send corrections to info@music98.news with the subject "Correction".

How the site is funded

music98.news may earn revenue from advertising, including Google AdSense, and from some affiliate links such as eligible ticket or music-service links. Those commercial relationships are separate from editorial decisions: payment or commission does not determine whether an artist is covered, what an article says, or how an editorial ranking is ordered.

Where a purchase link may generate a commission, we disclose that relationship close to the relevant action. More detail is available in the Advertising and affiliate links section of our Terms.

Who is behind it

music98.news is an independent project. Editorial, corrections, rights, advertising and privacy enquiries all have a direct contact route rather than an anonymous submission system.

Get in touch

News tips, press releases, corrections, rights questions, privacy requests and advertising enquiries can be sent to info@music98.news. The Contacts page lists the recommended subject line for each type of message.
