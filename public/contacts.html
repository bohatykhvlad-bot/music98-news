Contacts

Talk to us

Everything comes to one address, and a person reads it.

Write to info@music98.news. One word in the subject line routes your message, and the words are listed below. No forms, no ticket numbers.

News tips, leaks and corrections

Subject "Tip" or "Correction"

Send what you know and how you know it: a link, a screenshot, a date, a document. You do not have to hand over a finished story, and sources who ask not to be named stay unnamed.

If we got something wrong, tell us which sentence and what the right version is. We fix it and say that we fixed it, rather than swapping the text quietly.

If you are in a story

Subject "Reply"

If a piece concerns you, you can add to it, dispute it or correct it. Send the sentence and your version, and the response is published with the story. We may not always reach you before publication, but we do not ignore you after it.

Press releases and music submissions

Subject "Release"

Labels, distributors, PR and artists: send the release with its date, the artwork, the official streaming and video links, and the credit we should print. A private link is fine for an advance. If there is an embargo, put it in the first line with a time zone.

Not everything that arrives gets covered. Being on the list decides nothing. The release does.

Rights, credits and takedowns

Subject "Rights"

Articles use official promotional material, and we credit what we can trace. If a photograph is yours and the credit is missing or wrong, write with the page address. We correct the credit or take the image down, and we do not ask you to prove what is obvious. The procedure is set out in the Terms of Use.

Advertising and partnerships

Subject "Ads"

Direct placements and sponsored formats, with rates on request. Advertising never buys coverage, no advertiser reads a story before publication, and we do not sell links, guest posts or pre-written articles.

Privacy and data requests

Subject "Privacy"

Access, correction or deletion of anything we hold about you, the mailing list included. What we collect, and what we do not, is set out in the Privacy Policy.

What happens to your message

Every message lands in one inbox and is read. We answer what we can act on, we do not send an auto-reply promising a review, and we do not pass your address on. music98.news is an independent publication, and the address above is the only one we use.
