Contacts

Talk to us

Editorial, rights, privacy and commercial enquiries all go to one monitored address.

Write to info@music98.news. Put one of the subject words below in the subject line so the message can be handled quickly.

News tips and corrections

Subject "Tip" or "Correction"

For a news tip, include the source material or context that lets us verify it. For a correction, include the page URL, the statement you believe is wrong and the strongest source supporting the correction.

If you are mentioned in a story

Subject "Reply"

If a story concerns you or your organisation, you can send a factual response, clarification or correction. We review relevant replies and update coverage when new verified information changes the record.

Press releases and music submissions

Subject "Release"

Artists, labels, distributors and PR teams can send release information, official artwork, credits, release dates, streaming or video links and any applicable embargo. Submission does not guarantee coverage.

Rights, credits and takedowns

Subject "Rights" or "Copyright"

If you control rights to a photograph, artwork or other material used on music98.news and believe the credit or use should be corrected, send the page URL and relevant ownership or licensing information. We review requests promptly.

Advertising, partnerships and affiliate links

Subject "Ads" or "Affiliate"

Use this subject for direct advertising enquiries, sponsorship proposals, incorrect commercial disclosures, or problems with Ticketmaster, Apple Music or other affiliate links. Advertising and affiliate relationships do not buy editorial coverage or ranking placement.

Privacy, cookies and data requests

Subject "Privacy"

Use this subject for access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests, including newsletter data. Our current practices, including PurpleAds advertising, cookies, maps and concert location search, are described in the Privacy & Cookies policy.
