Privacy & disclosure

If you subscribe, we store only that email for the newsletter. The chart player may keep a little playback state in your browser. No ad trackers, no profiling, no selling data.

Apple Music. The 30-second clips, artwork and Listen on Apple Music buttons come from Apple. Play a preview or tap a badge and your browser talks to Apple directly. Apple then handles technical data (including your IP address) under the Apple Privacy Policy. We stream the clip; we never re-host the audio. Those badges are affiliate links (Apple Performance Partners): if you start a subscription we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. That never changes what we cover or how the Top 50 is ranked.

You can ask us to see, correct or delete a newsletter address. Data stored only in your browser clears when you clear this site’s data. Not aimed at children under 13.
