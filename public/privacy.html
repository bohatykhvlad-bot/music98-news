Privacy & Cookies

This policy explains what music98.news processes, how advertising and third-party services work, and the choices available to readers.

Who we are

music98.news is an independent music publication available at music98.news. Privacy questions and data requests can be sent to info@music98.news with the subject "Privacy".

Information we process

If you subscribe to the newsletter, we store the email address you provide so we can send the digest and process unsubscribe requests. We do not provide reader accounts, comments or public profile pages.

Like other websites, our hosting and security infrastructure processes technical request data such as IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, timestamps and security signals. This is used to deliver the site, diagnose problems and prevent abuse.

Cookies and local storage

music98.news uses browser storage for functional purposes such as interface settings, cached feed or chart data, and previously loaded preview information. Clearing this site's browser data removes those local items.

Third-party services used on the site may also use cookies, local storage or similar technologies for security, functionality, measurement, advertising and fraud prevention, subject to their own policies and your consent choices where required.

Google AdSense and advertising

music98.news has integrated Google AdSense and has submitted the site for Google's review. Advertising may be served after approval and according to Google's publisher and ad-serving rules.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, browser or device identifiers, cookie or local-storage information, approximate location derived from network data, ad requests and interactions in order to deliver, measure and protect advertising. Depending on your region and consent choices, ads may be personalised, non-personalised or limited.

Google explains how it uses data from sites and apps that use its services on its partner sites and apps page. You can manage Google advertising preferences in Google Ads Settings.

Where consent is required, Google advertising is subject to the consent controls available for that region. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, personalised advertising requires a Google-certified consent management platform that supports the applicable IAB Transparency and Consent Framework.

Concert search, maps and location

The Concerts section uses Mapbox for maps and location search and Ticketmaster for event information. If you choose "Use my location", your browser asks for permission first. The coordinates are then used to search for nearby concerts; we do not create a reader account or a personal location history from that request.

City searches and map requests may be processed by Mapbox. Concert queries may send a city, coordinates, radius or artist identifier to our server and Ticketmaster so matching events can be returned. Ticket links can contain affiliate tracking supplied through Ticketmaster or its affiliate partners, which may process referral and click information when you follow a ticket link.

Apple Music and embedded media

Artwork, previews and Apple Music links may load from Apple's services. Embedded or linked media from services such as YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Spotify, SoundCloud or Vimeo may cause those providers to receive the technical information normally sent when your browser requests their content. Their own privacy policies apply.

Services we rely on

Cloudflare provides hosting, delivery, security and server-side storage used by the site. Apple supplies catalogue metadata, artwork and music previews. Mapbox provides maps and geocoding. Ticketmaster provides concert and ticketing data. Resend delivers the newsletter. Google AdSense provides advertising services when enabled. In limited cases, public proxy services may be used to retrieve catalogue data when a direct browser request is unavailable.

Newsletter

The newsletter is sent only to addresses submitted for that purpose. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe method. We keep a subscriber address for as long as the subscription remains active or as needed to process an unsubscribe request.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing of personal information, or to request a portable copy. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

For requests, write to info@music98.news with the subject "Privacy". Advertising choices can also be controlled through the consent message shown where required and through the relevant provider settings.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose described above, legal requirements, security or dispute handling. Access to publishing and subscriber systems is restricted, and traffic to the site is encrypted in transit. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

The site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 where local law requires a higher consent age. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States and European jurisdictions. Those providers are responsible for the transfer mechanisms and safeguards applicable to their services.

Changes

We may update this policy when the site, advertising setup or service providers change. The date at the top identifies the current version.

Contact

Privacy and data requests: info@music98.news. Copyright, corrections, advertising and other editorial contacts are listed on the Contacts page.
