Privacy Policy

music98.news is an independent music publication. This page covers what we collect and how Apple Music is used on the site.

What we collect

If you subscribe, we store only the email address you submit, and only to send the newsletter. We do not use advertising trackers, we do not profile readers, and we do not sell data.

The charts player may remember playback in this browser. That stays on your device. Clearing this site's data removes it.

Apple Music

Previews, artwork and Listen on Apple Music buttons are provided by Apple. If you play a clip or open a link, Apple may receive technical data such as your IP address, under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Links to Apple Music are affiliate links. If you start an Apple Music subscription we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you.

Newsletter

The weekly digest is sent to subscribers only. Every email contains a one-click unsubscribe link; unsubscribing removes the address immediately and permanently. We never share the subscriber list with third parties.

Cookies & advertising

We currently run no third-party advertising scripts. If this changes, this page will be updated before any ads appear, including any consent options required in your region.

Your choices

To correct or delete a newsletter address, use the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or write to hello@music98.news with the subject "Privacy". This site is not directed at children under 13.
