Taylor Swift has released *The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore*, an expanded edition of her 2025 album that adds four new songs. The release arrived on September 25, 2026, with the original 12-track album followed by the new material on Apple Music. The Encore was announced on September 23, one day after Swift revealed "Patient Zero" as a new single. Apple Music lists the expanded edition at 16 songs and 41 minutes, with the original album on Disc 1 and the new material on Disc 2. The release keeps the original album intact while adding music written after its first release.

Swift said the new songs came from a trip to Sweden with Max Martin and Shellback, the producer and songwriter collaborators on *The Life of a Showgirl*. She said the trip was meant to celebrate the response to the album, but there was a studio nearby, and the three "wrote more songs." The original album was released on October 3, 2025. The Encore therefore adds material created after that album was finished rather than expanding its original recording sessions. Swift introduced the new edition on September 23, describing the songs as coming from gratitude for the response to the album.

The Encore cover uses a previously unseen image from the original *Showgirl* photo shoot by Mert Alas and Marcus Piggott. Swift appears in a metallic gold-and-silver fringe dress in front of a magenta curtain, with the photograph repurposed as the cover for the expanded edition. Variety reported that the new release continues the visual work the photographers created for the original album. No physical edition of *The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore* has been announced. Swift's official store separately offered collector editions of the "Patient Zero" single, rather than a physical version of the four-song Encore.

The release also sets up a new video for "Patient Zero." Swift directed the video and stars in it with Dakota Johnson, the actor, and Colin Farrell, the actor. Emmanuel Lubezki, the cinematographer, shot the video. The video is scheduled to premiere during the 2026 MTV Video Music Awards on September 27, 2026. Swift is also set to receive the inaugural Artist Director Honors at the ceremony. The video was teased on "CBS Mornings" on September 24, with footage showing Swift and Johnson together before Farrell appears.

The Encore arrives less than a year after *The Life of a Showgirl*. The expanded edition is available as a digital release, with Apple Music carrying all 16 songs. The four additions give the album a second disc without changing the original 12-song sequence, while "Patient Zero" leads the new material into its September 27 video premiere.
